2. Production-readiness & scale — real-world / magnitude assessment

Ready for the real world

  • Software correctness: 344 tests green, deterministic runtime, durable checkpointing, honest-unavailable everywhere (no silent fabrication in the runtime).
  • Security posture: real PQC, zero-trust gateway, signed identities, RLS, no committed secrets (.env/creds gitignored), SBOM + bandit gates.
  • Auditability: tamper-evident signed evidence chain + Annex-IV pack — genuinely regulator-facing, ahead of most commercial products.
  • Deployability: compose-up, shadow-mode, $0 trial; pilot runbook with a progressive-autonomy canary ladder + rollback.

NOT yet ready for scale / magnitude

  • Scale (G-066): single-worker, single-node. No incident-sharding router, no read-replicas/partitioning, no multi-node HA/failover, no load test at fleet magnitude. A 10,000-asset plant would not run on this as-is.
  • Real efficacy unproven (G-035/043): models are on proxy data. Real-world accuracy, drift, and false-alarm rates are unknown until a pilot re-fits on site telemetry.
  • Certification (G-011): the evidence machinery is real and self-attesting, but a notified-body/accredited certificate + CE marking + EU-database registration are not done (need a legal-entity provider).
  • Operator surface: thin frontend; no persona-shaped adoption UX yet (Stage 28 target).
  • Go-live safety wiring (R4/R5): live mTLS client-cert→peer binding + first-real-PLC sil_bridge hardening are wire-as-you-deploy items.
Honest scale verdict: production-grade engineering discipline, pilot-deployable, but not production-scaled. It is ready for a single controlled pilot line/site, not for enterprise-wide or multi-site magnitude. That is the correct place for it to be at GA — and the next gap to close.

8. Honest verdict — gamechanger? adopted easily? works efficiently?

Is it a gamechanger? Potentially, in a specific niche — not universally, not yet. The combination it ships (neutral + certifiable + PQC + SIL-safe + self-healing, all open) genuinely does not exist elsewhere, and the regulatory tailwind (EU AI Act high-risk from Dec 2027) is real and dated. But "gamechanger" requires market proof it does not have: no real deployment, no real-data efficacy, no certificate, no scale. It is a credible gamechanger candidate, not a proven one.

Will it be adopted easily? No — enterprise OT adoption is never easy. It is trust-gated, incumbent-defended, and slow (6–12 month cycles are normal). BUT the wedge lowers the barrier more than most: open-source + $0 shadow trial + HITL default + prevented-loss framing + no-data-leaves-site is about as frictionless as regulated industrial AI gets. Adoption will be earned pilot-by-pilot, not viral.

Will it work effectively / efficiently? As software, yes — it is real, tested, deterministic, and honest. As a real-world outcome engine, unproven until a pilot re-fits the models on real telemetry and publishes a real A/B. The Stage-6 simulated A/B is encouraging, not conclusive.

Bottom line: the rarest thing here is the honesty and depth of the engineering — most "industrial AI platforms" are demos with a governance slide; this is a governance-real system that discloses its own gaps (even G-082, the legacy fabrication, is ledgered not hidden). That discipline is itself the competitive edge. Convert it to a gamechanger by closing four gaps in order: real pilot (G-035/043) → scale (G-066) → certification (G-011) → adoption UX (Stage 28).
