6. The defining limitation, stated plainly

The discipline is production-grade; the evidence is not yet real-world. This system can be deployed into a supervised pilot tomorrow, and every safety, crypto, and governance layer is real — but no number in it has yet been earned against a real fleet, a real tariff, a real operator, or a real regulator. That is not a flaw in the build; it is the honest boundary of what free/local work can reach. Crossing it requires a pilot, a notified body, and production traffic — three external parties, not three more stages.
