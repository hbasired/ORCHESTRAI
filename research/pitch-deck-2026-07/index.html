01 — THE PROBLEM

Industrial autonomy is arriving faster than the trust to deploy it.

Operators are being sold robots and AI from a dozen vendors — with no neutral way to make them cooperate, prove they are safe, or satisfy a regulator. The result is stalled pilots and black-box systems no one will let near a SIL-rated actuator.

Seven pains, one missing layer

  • Labor shortage forcing automation before operators are ready for it.
  • Multi-vendor lock-in — every AMR / robot / PLC speaks its own dialect.
  • The integration tax — custom glue that diverges from the WMS/MES state.
  • Downtime + black-box PdM mistrust — nobody acts on a score they can't explain.
  • EU AI Act evidence burden — high-risk systems must log & prove every decision.
  • PQC / crypto lifecycle — today's signatures won't survive a quantum adversary.
  • Unexplainable autonomy — no audit trail = no deployment past a demo.

Why incumbents don't solve it

Fleet managers orchestrate one vendor's robots — orchestration is now a free commodity (InOrbit open-sourced OpenRobOps in Feb 2026). Industrial-AI suites (Siemens + NVIDIA) are powerful but vendor-locked. Sensor-PdM vendors sell scores, not governed action. Safety PLCs stop at the machine.

No one sells the neutral layer that sits above all of them and makes autonomous action trustworthy, provable, and legal. That is the gap.

A control plane that occupies a four-way intersection no competitor holds.

Autonomous × Certifiable × Neutral × Provable. The headline is the Causal Self-Healing Engine: the system doesn't just react to failures — it predicts them, reasons about the cause, formally verifies the fix is safe, and acts, leaving a cryptographically signed trail behind every decision.

06 — WHY NOW

Four clocks are striking at once — and they all favour a neutral trust layer.

⏱ Regulation

The EU AI Act's high-risk obligations land 2 December 2027. Every high-risk industrial AI system will need automatic logging, traceability, and a technical file. Almost no one is ready. We generate that evidence by construction.

🔐 Post-quantum migration

NIST's ML-DSA / ML-KEM standards (FIPS 203/204) are final; "harvest-now-decrypt-later" makes long-lived industrial evidence a target today. We are PQC-native, not PQC-retrofit.

🤖 Physical-AI capital wave

Humanoids are entering warehouses per-OEM and vendor-locked (Agility, Boston Dynamics, Figure). Mixed fleets need a neutral layer to speak every surface. Dedicated physical-AI funds are forming (BMW i Ventures $300M; KOMPAS €160M).

🔗 Protocols standardised

Agent-to-agent (A2A, 150+ orgs) and MCP (~97M installs, Linux Foundation) went mainstream — but industrial A2A endpoints are still rare. That's an early-mover surface we already occupy.

Orchestration became a free commodity in 2026 (InOrbit open-sourced OpenRobOps; Cisco acquired Galileo). That's not a threat — it removes our lowest-value work and vacates the field of independents right where trust, safety, and evidence remain unsolved.
