01 — THE MENTAL MODEL

One loop, engineered once, running across N domains.

Everything below is in service of a single idea — the Causal Self-Healing Engine. Instead of a dashboard that reacts, the system runs a closed loop that anticipates failure, reasons about its cause, proves the fix is safe, acts, and leaves cryptographic evidence. The same engine is re-used for each new domain (production line, supply chain, energy) — which is why breadth was cheap to add.

Production line live

RUL forecast → causal diagnosis → verifier → RL recovery + repair-robot dispatch.

Supply chain live

Multi-agent Contract-Net replenishment + disruption monitor + stochastic reorder.

Facilities / energy live

MILP peak-shaving & load-shifting vs a time-of-use + demand-charge tariff.

05 — REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

RL used where planning beats rules — and only when it provably does.

Where & why RL

Maintenance scheduling under crew contention and opportunistic batching is a sequential decision problem — exactly where a learned policy can outperform static thresholds by trading off future risk against present cost. That's the intervention step of the loop.

The honest two-attempt story

  • v0 — from-scratch PPO (actor-critic MLP 2×64, 24k steps). It learned but only tied the hand-rules. We reported the negative and kept rules as the default.
  • v1 — MaskablePPO (SB3-contrib) with action masking + a richer MDP (group batching, crew contention, opportunistic demand), 250k steps. It beat the best rule: −125.1 vs −137.4, 95% CI [6.0, 18.71], 36/50 paired wins.

How it's evaluated & deployed — safely

  • CRN-paired evaluation (common random numbers) so the RL-vs-rule comparison is a true paired test with a confidence interval, not a lucky seed.
  • Action masking encodes hard constraints directly into the policy's action space — it can't even propose an illegal action.
  • Shadow-mode deployment in the live runtime: the policy runs, logs its recommendation and its agreement with the rules, and never actuates. Promotion is gated by the autonomy ladder + human-in-the-loop.
  • The verifier/validator remain the shield — even a promoted RL action must pass the neuro-symbolic plan verifier and the SIL-rated executor.
Parameter tuning, in general: hyperparameters were chosen by grid/seeded search and reported in each model card (e.g. the demand LSTM's window/hidden/feature set; the RUL Transformer's heads/FFN/PE; the PdM recall-tuned threshold 0.779; PPO's step budgets). Model selection always used a held-out split with a single final eval — no test-set peeking — and the losing option (MLP over XGBoost, from-scratch PPO) is retained and documented, not hidden.
06 — THE TECHNIQUES

The methods that make the loop deep instead of shallow.

Learned causal discovery (PC algorithm)

Rather than hand-coding "vibration causes cracks", the PC constraint-based algorithm (causal-learn) recovers the structural causal model from data — skeleton F1 0.75, correctly finding the proximity hub. Diagnosis is discovered, not asserted.

Neuro-symbolic plan verification

A symbolic constraint engine checks every proposed plan against declarative safety/throughput contracts (crew capacity, throughput floor, SIL redundancy) and rejects unsafe ones. The neural policy proposes; the symbolic verifier disposes.

Counterfactual explainability (DiCE + SHAP)

Every decision carries a "why": exact SHAP top drivers + DiCE counterfactuals ("had torque been 8% lower, no maintenance"). An operator gets a reason, not a bare score — the trust the black-box PdM vendors can't give.

GraphRAG grounding

Answers are grounded in a real SOP corpus (bge-small retrieval) + a 1–2-hop ISA-95 Neo4j neighbourhood, with explicit citations — and an honest-empty "I have no evidence for that" when nothing grounds the question. No hallucinated operations advice.

MILP optimization (energy)

Peak-shaving + load-shifting is solved as a real mixed-integer program (scipy/HiGHS): minimise time-of-use energy + demand charge, subject to the production floor. Genuine optimization, not a heuristic.

Contract-Net consensus (supply chain)

Replenishment uses the classic Contract-Net Protocol — announce, sealed bids from observed supplier stats, min-cost award — with every award routed through the safety gate. Multi-agent coordination that's auditable.

Active diagnosis (information gain)

When the fault is ambiguous, the system selects the probe with maximum mutual information, does an exact Bayesian belief update, and commits only above a confidence threshold — else abstains. Diagnosis that knows when it doesn't know.

Capability tokens (safety)

Actuation is authorised by an unforgeable, action-bound, time-limited HMAC capability token minted at validation — defeating forged, stale (TOCTOU), and wrong-action decisions. Independently bypass-tested: 0 bypasses.

07 — IBM AGENT PRINCIPLES, KAGENTI & PROTOCOLS

Built to plug into the emerging enterprise-agent ecosystem — on our own spine.

The agent layer follows the principles and protocols that IBM, the Linux Foundation, and the cloud-native community are standardising — so the system is discoverable and governable inside an enterprise mesh, while keeping its own safety and evidence spine.

Protocols: A2A + MCP (and ACP, folded in)

  • MCP (Model Context Protocol) — our tools are exposed as MCP servers; the runtime consumes them as MCP-mediated tool calls (~97M installs, Linux Foundation).
  • A2A (Agent-to-Agent) — a signed agent card at /.well-known/agent.json + a JSON-RPC capability endpoint. External peers reach a deliberate subset of skills, never the full MCP tool surface (the trust asymmetry).
  • ACP (IBM's Agent Communication Protocol) — functionally folded into the same A2A foundation; we don't ship a third protocol.

The Kagenti cloud-native pattern (IBM / CNCF)

  • Dual identity — a SPIFFE X509-SVID for transport authentication (SPIRE-rotated), and ML-DSA-65 for evidence signing. The AgentCard binds both.
  • Framework-neutral AgentCard exported as the LF A2A-spec JSON and a Kagenti AgentCard-CRD — so the agent is discoverable inside a CNCF platform or IBM watsonx Orchestrate.
  • Live SPIRE server+agent issuing real SVIDs; zero-downtime rotation drilled (same identity, new serial).

09 — POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY

PQC-native, because industrial evidence must survive decades.

ML-DSA-65 signing

FIPS-204 lattice signatures sign every audit-chain row, agent card, ADR, and model card. Pluggable KeyProvider (software → HSM/Vault by config).

Hybrid ML-KEM-768 + X25519 TLS

FIPS-203 key encapsulation in a hybrid handshake on OpenSSL 3.5 — classical and quantum-safe at once, verified live with an ML-DSA-65 certificate.

SLH-DSA long-trust

FIPS-205 stateless-hash signatures for long-lived firmware/model-bundle trust; all model cards carry self-verifiable signed footers.

Why now, not later: "harvest-now, decrypt-later" means an adversary can capture today's classically-signed evidence and forge it once a quantum computer exists. For a signed audit trail that must remain trustworthy for the lifetime of an industrial asset, PQC is a build-time requirement, not a future upgrade. New signing code is post-quantum by rule; classical-only crypto is CI-blocked.
10 — FUNCTIONAL SAFETY

An LLM never touches an actuator un-gated. Full stop.

The planner ⟂ executor split

The LLM/agent is a planner only. Every actuator-bound action is described by a declarative safety contract (preconditions, invariants, SIL level) and must pass validator.validate(). The SIL-rated executor (sil_bridge) is the only emitter of an actuator command — and it re-validates from the contract + world-state, or demands a fresh capability token bound to that exact action.

Defence in depth

  • Capability tokens — forgery / TOCTOU / replay / wrong-action all rejected (independently bypass-tested, 0 bypasses).
  • Trace-pairing CI — every actuator span must be preceded by a safety.validate span, or the build fails.
  • STO / SS1 safe-torque-off & safe-stop paths on fail-safe, with signed audit rows.
  • IEC 61508 SIL ↔ ISO 13849-1 PL mapping; the sil_bridge is the certified-PLC integration seam.

13 — WHAT / HOW / WHY — THE DEEP QUESTIONS

The answers you'll be asked.

Why a self-healing loop instead of a dashboard or a chatbot?
Because value in industrial ops is prevented downtime, not surfaced charts. A dashboard tells you it broke; the loop predicts it, proves the fix is safe, acts, and proves it did — the difference between insight and outcome.
Why open source if you want to be funded?
The commodity layers (orchestration) are already free — fighting that is a losing game. Open-core drives adoption; the regulated must-pay layers (signed evidence, safety attestation, key custody) are the niche, so they're defensible. The moat is non-code: time-anchored signed evidence, the per-site data flywheel, certification, and trust.
Why should I believe the metrics?
Because they're labelled for exactly what they are, reproduced by an independent reviewer, and the losing results (from-scratch PPO tying the rules, the MLP losing to XGBoost) are reported, not hidden. A team that shows its negatives is telling the truth about its positives.
What's genuinely proven vs. what needs a pilot?
Proven: a working, theatre-free platform; the loop across three domains; real safety, PQC, and EU-AI-Act machinery; benchmark + simulation results. Needs a pilot: real-world A/B numbers, accredited certification, scaled load, and revenue. The gap is three external partners (a site, a notified body, traffic) — not more code.
How is this different from just calling an LLM?
An LLM is one component (the planner), and it is never trusted to act. It's wrapped in causal reasoning, a symbolic verifier, a SIL-rated executor with capability tokens, injection defences, and a post-quantum evidence chain. The LLM proposes; the safety and evidence spine disposes.
Why post-quantum crypto today — isn't that premature?
Harvest-now-decrypt-later. An industrial audit trail must stay trustworthy for the life of the asset; evidence signed classically today is forgeable once a quantum computer exists. Being PQC-native now costs little and removes a future migration nobody else has started.
