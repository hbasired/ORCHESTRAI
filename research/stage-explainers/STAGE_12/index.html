1. Why & what

The runtime needed a memory beyond a single run: a regulator-grade, tamper-evident record of every decision (EU AI Act Art. 12) and a real episodic/semantic memory so it can recall "what happened on this incident before." 3. Two design calls (research-grounded, honest)

  • HNSW, not ivfflat (research §19.2): the store is insert-as-you-go (a memory per incident), and HNSW handles incremental inserts + needs no training step + has higher recall, whereas ivfflat must train its lists and degrades under inserts.
  • Direct pgvector, not the mem0ai library (research §19.1): mem0ai needs an LLM for fact-extraction + imposes its own schema, conflicting with KB_14's mem0_memories contract + the offline/free constraint. We implement the contract directly with real embeddings.
  • Namespace isolation (NIST RMF): the adapter binds to an incident (+operator) context; a foreign tenant namespace raises CrossNamespaceAccessError before any I/O. Honest-unavailable everywhere (AuditChainUnavailable, EmbedderUnavailable, Neo4jUnavailable) — never a fake embedding or dropped audit row.
