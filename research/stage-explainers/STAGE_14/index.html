Stage 14 — A2A Protocol Surface CLOSED

Our agents get a signed identity + a safe federation boundary · ADR 2026-06-15_stage14_a2a_protocol.md · KB_16 · research §24.

1. Why & what

Stage 13.5 gave us a real ML-DSA-65 signer. Stage 14 uses it to build the external agent-to-agent (A2A) boundary: other organisations' agents can discover us (a signed agent card), verify who we are (post-quantum signature + pinned roots + revocation), and call a deliberate capability subset (JSON-RPC 2.0) — without ever touching our internal tools.

peer ──GET /.well-known/agent.json──> signed AgentCard (ML-DSA-65) ──verify(sig, pinned-roots, revocation, expiry) peer ──POST /a2a/v1/rpc {method}──> JSON-RPC dispatch ─> a2a.skills (forecast_oee, …) [MCP tools: -32601, refused]

External peers reach capabilities — never the MCP tool surface. The trust asymmetry is the security property.

2. The build

  • agent_card.py — KB_16 card schema; sign_card sets our ML-DSA-65 public key + signs the JCS (RFC 8785) canonical card-minus-signature; verify_card checks revocation → expiry → signature → pinned-roots (any failure → False).
  • server.py — /.well-known/agent.json (signed card) + /a2a/v1/rpc (JSON-RPC 2.0). Dispatches ONLY a2a.skills.SKILLS.
  • revocation.py (5-min poller, fail-safe: an unreachable list keeps the last cached set) · peer_state.py (active/quarantine/revoked) · skills/forecast_oee.py (real OEE — the exposed capability).
  • Migration 0007_a2a_peers; main.py mounts the routes. No new dependencies.

3. Why hand-rolled, not a2a-sdk (research §24)

a2a-sdk 1.1.0 requires httpx≥0.28.1 but our stack is pinned httpx 0.27.2 (shared by fastapi/starlette/mcp/langfuse — the same version-skew that bit us before) and it pulls google-api-core/protobuf. Our card is also PQC-specific (ML-DSA-65 fields). So we hand-roll a genuinely A2A-conformant surface (JSON-RPC 2.0 + /.well-known/agent.json) with our real ML-DSA-65 card signing — KB_16's documented fallback. a2a-sdk adoption is ledgered (G-070) for when the httpx pin is bumped. This is honest depth: real wire format, real PQC signatures, real federation flow — not a stub.
