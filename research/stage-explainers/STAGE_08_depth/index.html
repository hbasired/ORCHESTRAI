1. Why this deepening

The original Stage 8 was honest but shallow: a toy 1-layer LSTM on a near-trivial SimWorld signal (97.8% but easy), a hand-coded causal SCM (asserted, not measured), and no VERIFY step. The operator's mandate is depth + toughness. A SOTA research pass (research §16) grounded three honest, free/local/CPU deepenings of KB_25's predict → reason → verify loop.

A real benchmark number, a learned (not assumed) causal graph, and a working safety verifier.

4. VERIFY — neuro-symbolic plan verifier KB_25 step 3 — now BUILT

KB_25's loop is predict → reason → verify → intervene; VERIFY was PLANNED-only. backend/services/plan_verifier.py is the symbolic half of neuro-symbolic verification: the neural proposer (world-model / causal / RL) proposes a plan ⟂ this deterministic constraint engine disposes.

  • crew_capacity — no more simultaneous maintenances than crews (KB_25: "one crew").
  • maintenance_precondition — can't service a broken / already-in-maintenance machine.
  • throughput_floor — the plan must keep ≥ a floor fraction of stages online.
  • critical_redundancy (SIL) — don't take >1 critical machine offline at once.

HARD violations reject the plan before any actuator moves; SOFT ones warn. Composes with the Stage-17 functional-safety actuator wrapper (KB_17), which it predates.

5. Honest scope & limits

  • C-MAPSS validates the architecture, not the plant. FD001 is a public benchmark (single operating condition / fault mode); real-fleet generalisation still needs re-fit on real telemetry (G-035).
  • Causal discovery limits, stated not hidden: the ~3 K temperature-margin edges sit near the sensor-noise floor and are below PC's detection power; linear Fisher-Z cannot fully screen the semi-nonlinear rpm/wear coupling. The robust, claimable result is the proximity-hub property.
  • The verifier is a declarative predicate engine over a capacity-fraction throughput proxy — the dependency-light core of neuro-symbolic verification, not a full SMT/temporal-logic engine (a future deepening).
  • Our 13.80 RMSE is reported as-measured, slightly above the ~11–13 published SOTA — not claimed to beat it.
