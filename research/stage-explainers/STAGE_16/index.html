Stage 16 — VDA 5050 Robot Fleet CLOSED

A master controller for multi-vendor AGV/AMR fleets · VDA 5050 v2.1.0 over MQTT · ADR 2026-06-20_stage16_vda5050_robot_fleet.md · KB_12 · research §26.

1. Why & what

Our agent can now command a fleet of robots from any vendor through the open VDA 5050 v2.1.0 standard — the lingua franca between a master controller and AGVs/AMRs. The agent's self-healing decisions become real fleet orders (a graph of nodes + edges the robot drives), dispatched over MQTT.

decision ─> policy_query (MCP) ─> VDA order graph ─> [connection fresh? safety gate?] ─> uagv/v2/<mfr>/<sn>/order ─> AGV AGV ─> uagv/v2/<mfr>/<sn>/{state,connection} ─> master registry

No order leaves the master unless the AGV is verified ONLINE & fresh AND the order passes the safety gate.

2. The build (real, not mocked)

  • Real v2.1.0 schemas — the 6 official JSON schemas vendored from the VDA5050 repo at tag 2.1.0 (MIT). Caught that the repo's main is v3.0.0 (uses powerSupply vs v2.1.0 batteryState) — so we pinned the 2.1.0 tag. Pydantic models/ are generated from them.
  • master.py (Vda5050Master) — subscribes state/connection/factsheet/visualization; publishes order/instantActions; verifies connection freshness + ONLINE before any dispatch (anti-spoof).
  • safety/validator.py — every dispatch passes a structural + freshness gate emitting the safety.validate span (the SIL-rated validator is Stage 17).
  • policy_query.recommend_action → VDA-5050 routing when a fleet block is present (actions.py).
