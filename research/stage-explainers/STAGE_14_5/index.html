1. What a CTO checkpoint is

A periodic, read-only review by a fresh, different agent playing the CTO — it re-reads the whole system, hunts for theatre / gaps / cross-cutting risk, verifies the previous checkpoint's remediations were honoured, and routes new remediations forward as acceptance criteria on upcoming stages. It changes no code. CTO #3 covers everything since CTO #2 (which ended at Stage 10 + the depth-hardening pass).
