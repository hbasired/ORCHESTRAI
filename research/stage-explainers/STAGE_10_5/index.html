1. What this is

Every 10 stage-closures the project runs a CTO checkpoint — a read-only, whole-system review (persona cto-reviewer) that hunts for "looks-production-grade-but-is-theatre," verifies the prior checkpoint's remediations were honoured, and routes new remediations to upcoming task docs. CTO #2 fires at Stage 10.5, covering Stages 4–10 and the out-of-band Stages 6–10 depth-hardening pass (5 increments).

Verdict: ON TRACK, materially stronger than CTO #1 — with a growing review-independence debt and a proxy-vs-real-fleet validation ceiling.
