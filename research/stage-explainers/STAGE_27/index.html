2. The R4/G-4 closure — verified end-to-end

Before Stage 27 the A2A endpoint was RBAC-confined but not authenticated — an anonymous caller reached the read-only capability. Now (a2a/server.py): the verified client cert (X-Forwarded-Client-Cert, the mesh convention) yields the peer's SPIFFE ID, trust-domain/allowlist-checked, and that becomes the authenticated peer_id for governance. Proven with a REAL SPIRE-issued SVID and a genuine mutual-TLS handshake:

  • admitted a valid in-domain SVID authenticates and proceeds into the governance layer;
  • rejected a foreign-trust-domain SVID (spiffe://evil.example/…) is refused;
  • rejected an anonymous client (no cert) fails the handshake server-side (CERT_REQUIRED) — no peer identity is established.

Honest scope: this is load-bearing on the mTLS path (an mTLS-terminating front / sidecar supplies the cert). Without a front, the endpoint keeps the Stage-24 confinement fallback (anonymous L0, read-only) — named as weaker. Istio Ambient mesh mTLS = pilot/K8s.
