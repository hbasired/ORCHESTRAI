# Risk Register

> EU AI Act Article 9 evidence. Maintained per-component, per-stage. Append-only.

## Classification

This system is provisionally classified **HIGH-RISK** under EU AI Act Annex III (use as a safety component in manufacturing / operational decision-making with safety implications). Final classification is reaffirmed at every conformity-assessment milestone; until then this is the assumed worst case, so all controls are sized accordingly.

## Review cadence

- Re-reviewed at the close of every stage; new entries append at the bottom of the register table; the `Last reviewed` cell on existing rows updates if reviewed without change.
- Annually re-reviewed in full (the EU AI Act expects evidence of ongoing risk management, not a one-time exercise).
- Any new external integration (new dataset, new LLM provider, new MES system) requires a new row before the integration ships.
- Every CTO checkpoint (every 10 stages) refreshes the full register and produces `audits/CTO_<N>_review.md` flagging stale or missing rows.
